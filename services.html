What Is Ceramic Coating?

A permanent protective layer

Ceramic coating bonds chemically to your factory paint, forming a hard, transparent shell far more durable than wax.

Once cured, it protects against UV rays, oxidation, light scratches, bird droppings, road grime, and chemical contaminants. Its hydrophobic surface causes water and dirt to bead and roll off — keeping your car cleaner with far less effort.

UV & Heat Defense

Preserves your color and gloss through every season.

Hydrophobic Finish

Dirt and water slide right off for faster, easier washes.

Lasting Durability

Resists swirls and light scratches, outlasting wax by years.
