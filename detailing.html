Car Detailing FAQs

Common questions about what's included in a detail and how often to book one.

What's the difference between detailing and a car wash?

A car wash cleans the surface. Detailing is a thorough, hands-on process that deep-cleans, restores, and protects every surface inside and out — from vacuuming and shampooing the interior to decontaminating and sealing the paint.

What's included in interior, exterior, and full detailing?

Interior covers a full vacuum, cleaning and dressing of surfaces, upholstery or leather care, glass, and deodorizing. Exterior covers a hand wash, wheels and tires, contaminant removal, and a spray sealant or wax. The full detail combines both for a complete inside-and-out transformation.

How long does a full detail take?

It depends on the size and condition of the vehicle, but a full detail typically takes several hours. We'll give you a time estimate when we quote your vehicle.

How often should I have my car detailed?

Many owners detail every three to six months to keep their vehicle looking its best and protect its value, though it varies with how and where you drive. Regular detailing prevents grime, salt, and contaminants from causing long-term wear.

Can you remove pet hair, odors, and stains?

Yes. Our interior detail includes deep vacuuming, upholstery shampooing or leather conditioning, and deodorizing to tackle pet hair, odors, and most stains.

How much does car detailing cost?

Pricing varies by vehicle size and condition. Contact us with your vehicle details and what you'd like done, and we'll put together a quote.
