What Is Ceramic Coating?

A liquid nano-ceramic layer that bonds chemically to your factory paint, forming a hard, transparent shell far more durable than wax — guarding against UV rays, oxidation, scratches, and contaminants.

UV & Heat Defense

Preserves your color and gloss through every season.

Hydrophobic Finish

Dirt and water slide right off for faster, easier washes.

Lasting Durability

Resists swirls and light scratches, outlasting wax by years.

One-Step vs Two-Step Paint Correction

One-Step Paint Correction

A single pass with a machine polisher using a light- to medium-grit compound or polish. It removes minor swirl marks, light scratches, and surface oxidation. It's faster and more affordable, but won't address deeper defects. Best suited for newer vehicles or cars in relatively good condition that just need a refresh before coating.

Two-Step Paint Correction

Two separate passes — the first with a heavier cut compound to remove deeper scratches, swirls, and defects, followed by a finer polish to refine the finish and restore gloss. This is the most common professional approach, balancing defect removal with a clean, high-clarity result. Ideal for most vehicles that have noticeable paint issues.
