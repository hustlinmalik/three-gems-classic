Carbon vs Ceramic

Carbon Tint

A carbon-infused film that absorbs solar energy across the UV, visible, and infrared spectrum. It delivers a rich, matte, non-reflective look that stays color-stable — it won't fade or turn purple like old dyed films. Non-metallic, so it never blocks your signal. A great-value choice for drivers who want solid heat and UV protection with a factory-clean appearance.

Ceramic Tint

Our premium film, made with nano-ceramic particles engineered to reject infrared heat while staying optically clear. It rejects noticeably more heat than carbon at the same shade, keeping your cabin the coolest and most comfortable. Also non-metallic and signal-friendly, with excellent clarity and durability — the top pick for hot summers and long-term ownership.
