What Is Paint Protection Film?

PPF is a thick, clear urethane film applied over your factory paint. It absorbs the impacts of daily driving — rock chips, sand, road debris, and scratches — so they hit the film instead of your paint. Many films are self-healing: light swirls and marks disappear with heat from the sun or warm water.

Physical Barrier

A tough, sacrificial layer that takes the hits from rocks, debris, and everyday road wear.

Self-Healing

Minor swirls and scratches in the film vanish on their own with a little heat.

Virtually Invisible

Optically clear and applied to the panel edges, so it protects without changing your car's look.

Paint Protection Film FAQs

Common questions about paint protection film — and how it protects your vehicle.

What is paint protection film (PPF)?

Paint protection film is a clear, thick urethane film applied over your vehicle's factory paint. It acts as a sacrificial barrier that absorbs rock chips, scratches, bug acid, and road debris so they hit the film instead of your paint. Many films are self-healing, meaning light swirls and scratches disappear with heat from the sun or warm water.

How is PPF different from ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating is a thin liquid layer that adds gloss, UV protection, and a hydrophobic, easy-clean finish — but it won't stop a rock chip. PPF is a much thicker physical film that actually absorbs impacts. Many owners combine the two: PPF for impact protection, with a ceramic coating on top for shine and easier cleaning.

Will PPF damage my paint?

No. Quality PPF is designed to protect your paint and can be safely removed by a professional without harming the factory finish. By shielding the original paint underneath, it actually helps preserve your vehicle's appearance and resale value.

How long does paint protection film last?

With proper care, quality paint protection film lasts many years. Premium films are backed by manufacturer warranties of up to 10 years against yellowing, cracking, and peeling.

Is PPF worth it?

If you want to keep your paint looking new, PPF is the strongest protection available. It's especially worthwhile on new, leased, exotic, or long-term-keep vehicles, and for anyone who does a lot of highway driving. It's the only option that physically prevents rock chips and deep scratches, and it helps protect resale value.
